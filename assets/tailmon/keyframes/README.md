# Tailmon GPT Image key drawings

Each named PNG came from a separate built-in GPT Image call using the existing desktop Tailmon sitting drawing as an identity and style reference. Four independent drawings in a transparent 2×2 grid specify the anticipation, action, and recovery poses. The rendered animation PNGs are in [animations](../animations).

## Shared direction

Draw the same petite white kitten with blue eyes, yellow ridged gloves with dark claws, lavender ear tassels, pink inner ears, and a lavender-banded plume tail. Match the source ink, cel shading, proportions, and the three-quarter view from the left. Keep all four full-body sprites on a transparent background. Place one in each quadrant at the same scale and ground height, with room for both ears and the tail. Order the drawings top left, top right, bottom left, and bottom right. Draw original poses for the requested movement without lettering, effects, shadows, or panel borders.

## Action prompts

- **air-swat**. (1) tense upright facing slightly left, looking up at an imagined speck; (2) lift the far yellow glove and draw it back above the nose; (3) quickly sweep the same glove through the air above and toward the viewer's left, fully extended from the shoulder; (4) replant glove with a satisfied glance. No object or effects.
- **backward-stretch**. (1) sit with chin down and paws tucked; (2) plant both hind feet and arch chest backward, lifting both yellow gloves; (3) look upward with forelegs reaching above the chest and belly slightly forward, chin lifted and eyes closed; (4) relax back to the ground and lower both gloves.
- **belly-offer**. (1) lower from sitting onto one elbow; (2) turn onto the back and expose white belly, yellow gloves comfortably held up; (3) in the same belly-up pose, wriggle hips and tilt the head, with tail uncurling; (4) turn onto the side before getting up. Soft playful posture and correct four paws.
- **curious-head-tilt**. Start upright, tilt 12 degrees, increase to a 30-degree curious tilt with one brow raised, then straighten halfway. Keep the feet planted.
- **ear-radar**. (1) alert seated pause with one ear upright; (2) turn only the nearest tall ear outward with its tuft swinging; (3) the farther ear folds sharply forward toward a sound while the tall ear springs back; (4) both ears perk symmetrically, with a small curious head lean. This is independent ear articulation.
- **ear-scratch**. (1) seated, leaning onto one hip in preparation; (2) bring one clearly identifiable white rear foot to the base of the nearer ear, yellow front paws braced on the ground; (3) lift the white rear foot a little higher for the scratch, eyes closed with relief, head canted toward it; (4) lower the white foot and return toward sitting. Correct anatomy with two yellow front gloves and two white hind feet.
- **face-wash**. (1) seated, bring a single yellow paw to the mouth; (2) eyes shut, sweep the same paw under the nose and over the cheek; (3) touch the forehead between the ears with the same paw raised high; (4) lower arm slowly, eyes opening into a contented expression. One deliberate grooming stroke.
- **giggle**. (1) sit smiling with eyes gently narrowed; (2) lean into a small giggle and bring one yellow glove to the mouth, eyes squeezed shut and shoulders raised; (3) rocking back in a larger quiet laugh with eyes still closed, the other glove on belly; (4) lower glove and recover to fluffy relaxed smile. No text or effects.
- **happy-hop**. Compress into a crouch, leap with all four paws free, descend with the tail lifted, and land in a gentle squat.
- **knead**. (1) crouch gently with both yellow gloves on an imaginary soft surface; (2) press the nearer glove downward and lift the farther glove, eyes soft; (3) switch the two gloves so the farther one presses down and the nearer glove lifts; (4) lower both gloves and straighten into sitting. No cushion or other prop.
- **nose-sniff**. Tip the muzzle up, lean down and forward for a sniff, lift and reach forward for a second sniff, and straighten with wide eyes.
- **paw-shake**. (1) look down at the raised nearer glove with a puzzled expression; (2) extend that forepaw down and away from the body to shake off a drop, fingers pointing down and wrist loose; (3) snap the wrist upward briefly, eyes following it; (4) pull the glove back to the chest in relief. No drops or effects, just character motion.
- **paw-wave**. Sit and start to raise the nearer glove. Lift it to cheek height. Open it outward above the shoulder for the wave. Retract it toward the chest.
- **peekaboo**. (1) sit and raise both yellow gloves to her face; (2) cover both eyes behind the two gloves, keeping both ears visible; (3) move one glove aside and open one big blue eye toward the viewer; (4) lower both gloves, eyes wide and smiling. Make the reveal unambiguous.

- **pounce**. (1) tip sideways from sitting with paws tucked; (2) roll onto her side, ears relaxed and yellow gloves near the chest; (3) briefly roll belly-up with both white feet and yellow gloves visible, tail curling around; (4) use one front glove to push herself back up. Maintain head and garment details when rolling.
- **stargaze**. (1) sit still with head normal; (2) move gaze above the left ear, lift muzzle; (3) chin raised and large blue eyes visibly looking almost straight above, front paws drawn together, tail bracing on floor; (4) bring gaze partway down with a gentle smile. No stars or background.

Correctly show the back of the head in pose 2.
- **wink**. (1) friendly neutral seated smile; (2) tilt the head slightly with one eye halfway closed; (3) clear exaggerated one-eye wink, her other blue eye fully open, mouth smiling; (4) head rebounds and both eyes reopen. Keep the paws, ears and tail in all four cels.
